# Code of conduct

Hourmere has adopted the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1][covenant] as its code of conduct.
It applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and other project
channels) and when someone is officially representing the project in public spaces.

In short: be respectful and considerate, welcome people of every background and level of
experience, accept constructive feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the
community. Harassment, insults, sexualized language or imagery, personal or political attacks,
and publishing other people's private information without their permission are not
acceptable. The [full text][covenant] is the authoritative version.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to [hello@hourmere.com](mailto:hello@hourmere.com). Every report
is reviewed and handled fairly, and the privacy and security of the person reporting are
respected. Reports are handled in written English.

## Enforcement

The project maintainers (Cobaltmere, LLC) enforce this code of conduct. They may remove, edit or
reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that violate it. Consequences
follow the Covenant's [enforcement guidelines][enforcement]: depending on the severity, a
private correction, a warning, a temporary ban or a permanent ban from project spaces.

Hourmere currently has a single maintainer. If your report concerns the maintainer, you can
also report it to GitHub through its
[abuse reporting](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

[covenant]: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
[enforcement]: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines
